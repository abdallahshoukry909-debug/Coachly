FAQ

Questions

How do I make my tea?

Peel off the sealing film, pour in hot water up to the line, stir for a few seconds and enjoy. It's ready in about 1 minute.

Is there sugar in it?

No. I CUP contains only real black tea leaves — no sugar, no artificial colors and no preservatives. Add sugar or honey if you like it sweet.

Will tea leaves get into my drink?

No. The leaves sit under a filter paper inside the cup, so your tea stays clear.

What's in a pack of 10?

10 cups and 10 lids in a clear tube, with brewing steps and nutrition facts in Arabic and English on the label.

What's the industrial pack?

Ten retail tubes (100 cups) shrink-wrapped together — one easy unit for offices, cafés, distributors and wholesalers.
